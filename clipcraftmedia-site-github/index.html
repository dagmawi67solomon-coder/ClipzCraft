Before you message us

What platforms do you clip on?

TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and X. Your plan decides how many accounts you get on each.

How is "viewership doubling" measured?

We compare your average concurrent viewers in the 30 days before you signed up to your most recent 30 days. Once that average has doubled, your next billing cycle reflects the new rate.

Can I switch from Solo to Multi later?

Yes. You can move up a plan at any time; the new rate applies from your next billing date.

Do I need to edit anything myself?

No. Send us access to your VODs and we handle editing, captions and posting on every account.

Is there a contract?

No. Both plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime before your next billing date.
